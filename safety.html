Community safety

Buy and sell safely.

Meet smart

Meet in a public, well-lit place. Take a friend if possible and inspect an item before payment.

Pay carefully

Do not send deposits to strangers. Agree on the full price and payment method before meeting.

Keep your details safe

Use WhatsApp for item enquiries, but never share bank PINs, passwords, one-time codes, or personal documents.

Report concerns

Do not proceed with a transaction that feels unsafe. Contact the platform admin if a listing is misleading.
